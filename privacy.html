Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

SnapDrive is a browser extension for taking screenshots and recording your screen. It saves what you capture to cloud storage that you choose and connect: Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive or Dropbox.

What SnapDrive does with your data

  • Screenshots and recordings are processed on your own computer. They are sent only to the cloud account you connected, and only when you click Upload (or when you turn on automatic upload for recordings). SnapDrive has no servers of its own. We never receive, see or store your captures.
  • Cloud sign-in. When you connect a cloud, you sign in directly with Google, Microsoft or Dropbox. SnapDrive receives an access token that it keeps only in your browser's local extension storage. The token is used only to create folders, upload your files and create share links. You can disconnect at any time in Settings, which deletes the token.
  • Limited access.
    • Google Drive: SnapDrive uses the drive.file permission. It can only access files that SnapDrive itself created, never the rest of your Drive.
    • Dropbox: SnapDrive uses an "App folder", so it can only access its own folder.
    • OneDrive: Microsoft does not offer a folder-only permission for this, so SnapDrive requests the Files.ReadWrite permission. SnapDrive only uses it to create a SnapDrive folder, upload your captures into it and create share links for them. It does not read, change or delete any of your other files.
  • Account details. Your account's email address, name and profile picture are read only to show which account is connected. They are kept in local extension storage.
  • Settings and history. Your settings and a list of your recent uploads (file name, link and a small thumbnail) are stored in your browser. Settings may sync between your own browsers through your browser's built-in sync.
  • Screen and microphone. SnapDrive accesses your screen, microphone or system audio only after you choose to capture or record. If you turn on "Instant whole-screen capture", screen access stays open until you turn it off, and the browser shows a visible "sharing your screen" indicator the whole time.

What SnapDrive does not do

  • It does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • It does not use your data for any purpose other than the screenshot and recording features described above.

SnapDrive's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • Click Disconnect in SnapDrive Settings to delete the stored sign-in token.
  • You can also remove SnapDrive's access from your account at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, Microsoft at account.live.com/consent/Manage, Dropbox at dropbox.com/account/connected_apps.
  • Uninstall the extension to remove all of its local data.
  • Files you uploaded stay in your own cloud storage until you delete them there.
