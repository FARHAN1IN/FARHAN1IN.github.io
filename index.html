Privacy policy

Screenshots and screen recordings, saved to your own cloud

SnapDrive is a free browser extension. Capture any area of your screen, mark it up, record video, and upload to Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox in one click.

Add to Chrome — it's free
📸 Capture any areaPress Alt+Shift+S to freeze your whole screen, then drag to select.
✏️ AnnotateArrows, boxes, text, highlighter, and pixelate to hide private info.
🎥 Record videoA tab, a window or your whole screen, with mic and system audio.
☁️ Your cloudGoogle Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox. The share link is copied for you.

Private by design

SnapDrive has no servers. Your screenshots and recordings go only to the cloud account you connect, and only you can see them. No tracking, no ads. Read the privacy policy.
